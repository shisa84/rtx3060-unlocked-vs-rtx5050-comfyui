Who wins each workflow?

On the stock profile (170 W), the 3060 runs its fans at full speed, locked or unlocked: 84 to 99% locked, 91 to 99% unlocked. On the optim profile, locked or unlocked, they stay at 71 to 76%, against 53 to 70% for the 5050.

What unlocking buys you

time saved at the same power limit

For everyday use, the 3060 unlocked optim. For speed, the unlocked 3060 at 170 W.

Unlocked optim uses less energy than the 5050 on all three workflows, stays around 70 °C and only loses time on LookSheets. Unlocked at 170 W is the fastest on Z-Image and MiniMax, at the cost of 80 to 83 °C and fans at full speed. The locked 3060 on the stock profile has no upside: unlock it at the very least.

Measurement limits

  • LookSheets and MiniMax: one run per setup. A gap of a few percent can come from normal run-to-run variation.
  • Locked optim was measured on two days: config 2 session on November 11 (Z-Image, LookSheets), separate run on November 13 (MiniMax). Same profile both times.
  • Z-Image: average of runs 2 to 5. For locked optim, runs 3 to 5, since run 2 was abnormally long (32 s).
  • Energy is for the graphics card alone, as read by nvidia-smi, not the whole PC. The 3060 also drives the display, about 29 W at idle, included in its readings.
  • The 5050 settings were not recorded.
Raw data: ComfyUI benchmarks\ folders (gpu_log.csv, history.json, summary.csv, config.txt). ComfyUI launched with --lowvram on both cards.
